No paperwork, no payment.

Plenty of customers won't pay an invoice that arrives without its proof of delivery or timesheet. Xero lets you attach files by hand, but nothing stops an invoice going out without them. One missed file means a rejected invoice, a resend and a later payment.

How it works

Set it up once. After that it works on every invoice for the customers you flag.

  1. 1

    Flag the customers who need paperwork

    Mark the customers who always need proof of delivery, a timesheet or a signed job sheet. Everyone else is left alone.

  2. 2

    Invoices wait until the files are there

    When you approve an invoice for a flagged customer, it's held until the supporting files are attached. If something is missing, you get an alert.

  3. 3

    Sent from your own Outlook

    The invoice and its files go out from your own mailbox, with a copy in Sent Items, and the invoice is marked as sent in Xero.

Questions

Can't I already attach files in Xero?

You can, by hand. The problem is that nothing stops an invoice going out without them. Attachment Guard holds invoices for flagged customers until the files are there, so they can't be forgotten.

Why send from Outlook instead of Xero?

Your customer gets the email from your own address, replies come straight to you, and you keep a copy in Sent Items like any other email.

Do I need to change how I work in Xero?

No. You keep creating and approving invoices in Xero as usual. Attachment Guard only steps in for the customers you've flagged.
